# Terms of use — template (needed for subscriptions, UGC, AI output)

Apple: apps with auto-renewing subscriptions must link Terms (or Apple's standard EULA) in the app and in the App Store description. UGC apps must have terms that forbid objectionable content and explain moderation. This is a starting point, not legal advice.
